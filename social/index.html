PwnZ Community Rules

Respect people. No hate, harassment, threats, stalking, impersonation or targeted abuse. Treat disagreements constructively.

Share your own content. Post only games, clips, code, music and imagery you have permission to use. No piracy, illegal material, graphic violence or sexual exploitation.

Protect privacy. Do not post private data or credentials. Respect blocks and reports. No spam, bots, scams or deceptive promotions.

Play fairly. No cheating, match manipulation or harassment of players. Tournaments, prizes, paid access and donations are not active until separately announced with rules.

Community safety. Content can be reported and moderated, and violations may lead to account restrictions. PwnZ Member status does not grant Game Creator or Gamer approval.

Identity. Your PwnZ name is a public gaming persona. Eden ownership is verified privately and never established by typing someone else's RealID.

Renewal. PwnZ-only accounts reaffirm this agreement every 30 days. Existing verified Eden RealID users follow their current Light of Hope agreement instead. You may browse permitted public content without accepting.
