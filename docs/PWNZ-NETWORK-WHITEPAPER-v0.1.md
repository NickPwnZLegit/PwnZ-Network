# PwnZ.live Network
## Foundational Whitepaper v0.1 · October 30, 2026

**Status:** Draft proposal. Platform architecture, game rights, subscription prices, token utilities, release dates, and reward mechanics remain subject to testing and approval.

### 1. Mission
PwnZ.live is a browser-first gaming and creator network intended to unite classic-style multiplayer experiences, legal game preservation and creator publishing, interactive livestreams and community discussion. Connected to the Light of Hope ecosystem's empathy-first philosophy, it aims to let people connect, play, create and support each other without requiring crypto ownership simply to socialize.

### 2. Ecosystem and identity
PwnZ provides distinct gamer and creator usernames that do not have to match an Eden RealID. Verified users may securely connect an EdenNetwork/Light of Hope account to PwnZ without publicly publishing biometric identity, wallet details or internal account identifiers. New users can establish a PwnZ account before applying for ecosystem verification. A shared Messenger of Light should connect verified members across Light of Hope, EdenNetwork, the LOH meme community, PwnZ and future EnderNet, with explicit consent and unchanged participant, block and agreement protections. Cross-site single sign-on is future work; independent logins are not the same as SSO.

### 3. Three principal community roles
**PwnZ Member (default):** Every user begins here, receives a PwnZ Member badge upon successful profile creation, and can build their own social profile, post authorized content, follow or add members, react with hearts, comment, watch Twitch and YouTube content, and participate in approved open community areas. Game access, subscriptions and certain interaction tools may have account/age restrictions.

**PwnZ Gamer (application and approval):** A member seeking a streamer or gaming-content creator identity applies with a short description and samples. Approved Gamers can build a channel presence, link verified Twitch/YouTube profiles, share livestreams and uploaded or linked gaming clips, and receive eligible tips and community engagement after payment compliance is in place.

**Game Creator (application and approval):** A member who develops or legally ports game software submits a description, source/rights evidence, game-content licenses, test builds and required technical/security documentation. Approval is required for hosting, distributing, enabling paid play or opening matchmaking. Old source code alone never grants rights to original artwork, maps, audio, names or other protected game assets. Approval is revocable.

These are roles and privileges, not separate mandatory identity accounts. Users may later hold more than one approved role subject to the final policy.

### 4. Social foundation and discovery
PwnZ profiles may contain an avatar, gamer bio, approved streaming channels, video links, authored posts and activity badges. The social network should provide public creator profiles, Members of PwnZ connections, follow/add member workflows, a chronological feed, comment threads, hearts, moderation reports and notifications. The connection graph must not make private Eden RealID metadata public. Future Alliance-style groups and communities can carry friends, creator circles, game communities and tournaments.

### 5. Video, Twitch and uploads
Twitch broadcasts can be embedded on PwnZ using Twitch's official player and chat under its embedding policies. YouTube videos can be shared through link previews or authorized embeds; their owners' embedding restrictions apply. Direct upload of clips or videos requires signed storage uploads, malware/abuse screening, rights complaints, size quotas, retention rules, accessible controls and a moderation queue. Game creators may submit legally permitted code/builds to a separate approval pipeline, never straight to live game execution.

### 6. Subscriptions and support
Public social viewing, posting and creator interaction are intended to have a free path. A **future monthly Arena subscription** can unlock licensed hosted browser games, multiplayer and eligible tournament participation. A separate **future creator subscription/fan membership** may let users subscribe to approved Game Creators for additional creator content or access, subject to clear benefit descriptions, prices, cancellations and refunds. Optional creator donations and tips may support Gamers and other eligible creators once approved payment processing, tax, fraud prevention and creator payouts are ready. Access should never depend on purchasing tokens merely to join the community.

### 7. Game publishing and tournaments
The gaming architecture envisions WebAssembly/WebGL or other secure browser-compatible game clients, dedicated multiplayer servers, game rooms, match discovery, seasonal maps, scoreboards and moderation. Initial releases should use original or clearly licensed content, with code and asset rights verified. Future skill-based tournaments might award funded cash or token rewards subject to age, geographic eligibility, contest law, anti-cheat, published rules, tax reporting, payout controls and legal review. No prize amounts, return rates or access dates are guaranteed.

### 8. PwnZ token and interconnected networks
The PwnZ token has already been created according to the project's history; its exact chain, contract and supply must be verified before the website provides onchain representations. Planned token utilities may eventually include approved creator features, certain ecosystem rewards and token community governance, subject to compliance. Light of Hope's planned assets include HOPE utility, EDEN identity/governance and LOH meme/community. Potential reviewed PwnZ/HOPE, PwnZ/EDEN, PwnZ/LOH or stablecoin liquidity links remain concepts, **not operating pools or price commitments**. Light of Hope's present stated pool focus is USDC/HOPE and HOPE/EDEN. Wallets, tokens and pools are independent of private messaging or gameplay simulation.

### 9. Safety, privacy and governance
An empathy-first community still requires enforceable rules: anti-harassment, copyright reporting, illegal content restrictions, spam and bot mitigation, age-sensitive protection, content moderation, blocks and appeals, accessible privacy controls, data minimization and deletion/retention choices. Private Messenger of Light threads are limited to eligible participants; this is not a claim of end-to-end encryption. Eden ownership checks must not expose internal user IDs or biometric identifiers. Administrator-approved role transitions, game submissions, payments and dispute reviews should have audit logs.

### 10. Delivery milestones
**Phase A: Social TestNet.** Secure identity, PwnZ member profiles, role applications, follows/add member, posts/comments/hearts, shared Messenger eligibility and Twitch/YouTube embedding. Moderation and security-rule verification must precede public accounts.

**Phase B: Creator Network.** Approved Gamer and Game Creator workflows, content upload moderation, creator directories, authorized game builds and creator membership/payment infrastructure.

**Phase C: Arena TestNet.** Legally distributable browser games, subscription entitlement checks enforced by servers, sessions and multiplayer infrastructure.

**Phase D: Tournaments and Interoperability.** Competition systems, vetted prizes, cross-network features and explicitly approved token utilities/pools.

### 11. Open decisions
Subscription price and billing model; whether fans subscribe per creator or to a network bundle; game creator revenue shares; approved game platforms and technical standards; PwnZ token chain, address and supply; global launch jurisdictions; moderator staffing; age limits; eligibility for donations and tournament rewards; and governance of game approvals.

### 12. Foundational principle
PwnZ makes social belonging the entrance, creative contribution the engine and gaming competition a future destination. Its Light of Hope connection should expand empathy, transparency and accessibility, rather than requiring anyone to invest or buy a token to participate.
