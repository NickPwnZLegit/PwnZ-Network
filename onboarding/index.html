What works today, and what's coming

Current sign-in: Account authentication uses your registered email and password. Some network integrations and Firebase permissions are still undergoing verification. Registration or profile features may be temporarily unavailable on a site.

Planned secure sign-in: After the shared RealID ownership resolver is validated, returning members will enter Eden RealID + password. First-time entry to another network will also verify their existing Eden RealID, email and password. These screens are not yet universally active.

Never enter your password anywhere except the official network sign-in form. This guide does not collect credentials, biometrics, wallet keys, or personal identity documents.

01 · Brand-new to the ecosystem

Create one account, not three. Choose the network where you want to begin.

💚 Starting at Light of Hope

  1. Open Begin Journey → Create Account.
  2. Choose your unique Eden RealID and enter an email and password.
  3. Finish account activation and read the community agreement when prompted.
  4. Customize your Light of Hope profile, then explore the social network.
Go to Light of Hope

✧ Starting at EdenNetwork

  1. Open Eden RealID account → Create.
  2. Choose an Eden RealID and your private email/password, when enrollment is available.
  3. If the registrar is paused, create your account at Light of Hope instead, then return with the same credentials.
  4. Complete the shared community agreement where prompted before member-only participation.

ZK uniqueness and biometric enrollment are research features, not part of current sign-up.

🎮 Starting at PwnZ

  1. Open Social → New ecosystem member → Create account.
  2. Choose an Eden RealID and enter an email/password.
  3. After the account is activated, reserve your unique PwnZ Gamertag on your PwnZ profile.
  4. Review any required agreement, customize your profile, and create posts when backend permissions allow.
Go to PwnZ sign-in

02 · I already have an Eden RealID

Do not create a second Eden RealID. The same owner account should follow you between networks.

💚 First visit to Light of Hope

  1. Choose Login, not Create Account.
  2. For now, enter the email and password associated with your existing Eden account.
  3. Once account ownership is checked, complete any pending profile activation and agreement.
  4. Use your Eden RealID as your ecosystem profile identity.

✧ First visit to EdenNetwork

  1. Open Eden RealID account → Existing sign-in.
  2. Use the original registered email/password during this transition.
  3. Complete any outstanding account activation or agreement step.
  4. When supported and verified, return later using Eden RealID/password.

🎮 First visit to PwnZ

  1. Open Existing Eden RealID on the PwnZ Social sign-in panel.
  2. Use your existing email/password; PwnZ checks the signed-in account's Eden ownership.
  3. Choose your unique PwnZ Gamertag on first activation, without replacing your Eden RealID.
  4. Customize your PwnZ profile. The future display-name choice will let you show your Eden name or Gamertag on PwnZ.

Common questions

Will I lose my Eden RealID if I choose a PwnZ name?

No. Eden RealID is the shared identity; the gamertag is your PwnZ-only public name. Future gamertag NFT trading at a proposed $9.99 price remains a separate, unreleased phase.

Why does the sign-in still ask for email?

Firebase Authentication currently uses email and password. The planned Eden RealID/password interface needs a secure private resolver, a tested ownership check, and migration of account rules before it can replace that flow.

What about community agreements?

The network is transitioning to one shared 30-day agreement. Until the coordination is fully deployed, follow only the agreement that your signed-in site actually presents. Do not make additional accounts to bypass renewal.

Is human uniqueness verification active?

No. Hope Labs is researching a privacy-preserving ZK human-uniqueness ledger. It will not require publishing legal identity, sensitive biometrics or your private account ownership on a public ledger. ANNEAL remains a research support layer and never decides identity or account access.

Are PwnZ Gamertag NFTs available?

Not yet. Future museum display, collectible badges, LightVault linking, NFT minting and marketplace trading need separate implementation, security reviews, and legal readiness before launch.

Having trouble? If sign-in works on one network but not another, avoid registering again. Check the exact email used for the original account, use that site's password-reset process if necessary, and report the network and error message without sharing passwords.
