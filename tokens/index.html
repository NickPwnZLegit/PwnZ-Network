PwnZ Network · Token policy

Two tokens.
One gamer ecosystem.

PwnZ uses the HOPE utility token and future PWNZ gaming/creator token. These are the network's intended tokens, not an active exchange.

HOPE

Shared Light of Hope ecosystem utility: future community support, creator interactions, and approved payments.

Lightchain AI contract not yet deployed or verified. No balance or transfer service activated.

PWNZ

Future PwnZ gaming utility, creator participation, tournaments, and approved in-game features.

Contract, marketplace, wallet permissions and pricing remain unverified and disabled.

No purchase required for ordinary social access.

This page does not request wallet signatures or execute token swaps, grants, balances, subscriptions, or gameplay payments. All real financial features require separate reviewed releases.
