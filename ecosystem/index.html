PwnZ is the planned gaming and creator token within the interconnected Light of Hope community. Our long-term goal is coordinated identity, community participation, optional token utilities and responsibly developed liquidity connections, without requiring visitors to use crypto to socialize.

PwnZ token · created

PwnZ Network

The PwnZ token has been created. Supported blockchain, verified contract address, tokenomics, wallet display, pools and exchanges are not yet independently verified here, so this page does not provide trading buttons or token price claims.

Utility · future

Gamers & creators

Proposed token uses include creator recognition, eligible tournament rewards, NFT collections and authorized community features, subject to launch readiness and legal review. Subscriptions and cash prizes remain off.

Connected assets · future

HOPE, EDEN & LOH

HOPE is intended for utility and donations; EDEN for verified identity and governance; LOH for the meme/community ecosystem. Separate contracts, token statuses and requirements must be verified independently.

Infrastructure · planned

EnderNet and network integration

EnderNet and future blockchain/settlement options are part of the longer-term roadmap. No currently operational shared blockchain is implied. EdenNetwork governance and cross-site community tools will have separate readiness checks.

Potential inter-ecosystem liquidity

Exploratory future liquidity may connect PwnZ with HOPE, EDEN, LOH or supported stablecoins, as approved. The current Light of Hope direction prioritizes USDC/HOPE and HOPE/EDEN; any new cross-token pool requires a separate decision, chain deployment, audited contracts, sufficient liquidity and risk disclosure. No PwnZ trading pair is being represented as launched.

Important: Liquidity pools are not guarantees of token value or investment returns. No staking APR, token airdrop amount, prize eligibility, liquidity funding or launch date is promised. Verify all addresses before integrating wallets.

Social worlds, one Messenger of Light

People will be able to move between Light of Hope, PwnZ, EdenNetwork, the LOH meme community and EnderNet when available. Messenger of Light is designed to preserve conversation history across worlds, using verified account identity and consent. PwnZ public usernames remain distinct from Eden RealID.
